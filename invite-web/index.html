PartyPlus FAQ

A few quick answers for hosts planning birthdays, cookouts, potlucks, family gatherings, holiday parties, game nights, and casual events.

What is PartyPlus?

PartyPlus is a party planning app that helps hosts create invites, track RSVPs, and organize what guests are bringing.

Can PartyPlus help with potlucks?

Yes. PartyPlus includes a shared bring-list so guests can claim items or suggest something they want to bring.

Can I use PartyPlus for birthdays and cookouts?

Yes. PartyPlus is designed for birthdays, cookouts, family gatherings, holidays, potlucks, game nights, and casual events.

Does PartyPlus track RSVPs?

Yes. Guests can RSVP so the host can see who is coming.

What problem does PartyPlus solve?

PartyPlus helps keep party details, RSVPs, guest counts, and bring-list items together instead of scattered across group texts and messages.
